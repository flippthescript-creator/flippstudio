01 / Profile

Artistic Statement & Perspective

My work revolves around examining the paradoxes of living today: the jarring collision of digital acceleration with private isolation, the tension between cosmic insignificance and everyday human empathy, and the unnoticed poetry within utilitarian urban streetscapes.

Whether analyzing Spinoza’s rationalism through a modern Cognitive Behavioral Therapy lens, wrestling with Camusian absurdism through contemporary pop culture, or walking the Midwestern pavements with a camera pointed at rusted steel and layered stickers, I seek out honesty and emotional lucidity.

"To truly cope with the very real, existing ache of grief by dismissing it is nothing, if not a lie... yet we can acknowledge that this doesn't negate our humanity."
